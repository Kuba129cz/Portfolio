Models for Forecasting Photovoltaic Power

This project presents a set of predictive models for forecasting the electrical power output of a photovoltaic power plant. Several mathematical models for short-term PV power forecasting were developed, ranging from simple baseline approaches to advanced architectures based on neural networks.

Model performance strongly depends on the accuracy of meteorological forecasts, which play a key role in short-term photovoltaic power prediction. The most accurate model achieved a normalized mean absolute error (nMAE) of approximately 6.5% during periods of non-zero power production.

The resulting model provides direct practical value for photovoltaic power plant operators, particularly for optimizing production forecasts and supporting financial planning under current European electricity spot market conditions.

Live Forecasting Model

The model provides power output predictions for the next five days, which corresponds to the availability horizon of numerical weather forecasts. In addition, it supports retrospective forecasting using historical meteorological data, enabling direct comparison between predicted and actual power production.
